# Nature Starter v0.1

Nature Starter v0.1 is original pixel artwork generated locally for TinyFarm. No external artwork, tracing, or downloaded assets are used.

## Source

- Production sheet: `res://art/environment/nature/nature_starter_v01.png`
- Dimensions: 256×64 px, RGBA, transparent background
- Logical grid: 16×16 px
- Review preview: `res://art/environment/nature/nature_starter_v01_preview.png` (8× nearest-neighbor enlargement with review grid)
- Palette: `res://art/palettes/tinyfarm_v01.gpl` and `res://art/palettes/tinyfarm_v01.txt`
- Deterministic generator: `res://tools/generate_nature_starter.gd`
- Atlas resources: `res://resources/atlases/nature_starter/`
